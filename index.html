You deserve good things.

University is a new chapter, and I hope it brings you kind friends, exciting opportunities, late-night laughs, and every success you’re capable of. I hope you keep choosing the things that make you feel like yourself.

This note is not asking for anything. It’s just a wish for you: keep growing, keep laughing, and keep becoming the version of yourself you love.

wishing you the best, zkiko
